About Nerima

Design that starts at the drawing board.

I graduated from Kyambogo University with a Bachelor of Interior and Landscape Design, then completed two internships at Meek Construction Company Limited. There I worked directly with clients on TV wall units, kitchen cabinets, living rooms and bedrooms, preparing floor plans, site plans and 3D designs for review.

I now work through Neri Creations & Interiors from Kasangati, Nangabo Road, Kampala. The practice covers home renovations, decor, lighting and painting, furniture design, 3D visuals, curtains and cushions, house planning and landscaping. I develop custom pieces from a blank sheet, check proportions on site and choose finishes and plants that suit the client and the place.

“A good design is one you can build, afford and still love a year later.”

How it works

Four clear steps

A simple, transparent process from first conversation to drawings you can build from.

02

03

Customise

04

Hand over

You receive clear drawings and a palette you can take to a builder or installer.
