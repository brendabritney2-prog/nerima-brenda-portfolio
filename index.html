About Nerima

Design that starts at the drawing board.

I graduated from Kyambogo University with a Bachelor of Interior and Landscape Design, then completed two internships at Meek Construction Company Limited. There I worked directly with clients on furniture and interiors, from TV wall units and kitchen cabinets to floor plans, site plans and 3D designs.

I like customising. That means starting with a blank sheet, developing a concept from scratch and making pieces that are not already on the market. I keep an eye on current trends, but the final decision always belongs to the person who will live with the space.

“A good design is one you can build, afford and still love a year later.”
