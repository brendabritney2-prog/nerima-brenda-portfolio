## Dimension references

Layouts were checked against published anthropometric and interior-design guidance, including:

- Ernst Neufert, *Architects' Data*
- Julius Panero and Martin Zelnik, *Human Dimension and Interior Space*
- NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines
- EN 12464-1 / CIBSE lighting guidance for residential task lighting

### Formulas and rules applied

- **Percentile rule (Panero and Zelnik):** dimensions where a person reaches are sized to the 5th percentile (the smallest user); dimensions where a person or object must clear are sized to the 95th percentile (the largest user).
- **Seat height** = 5th-percentile popliteal height + 38 mm for shoes and clothing, approximately 430 to 450 mm.
- **Dining table top** 750 to 760 mm, leaving about 300 mm of thigh gap above a 450 mm seat.
- **Worktop** approximately 900 mm (50 to 100 mm below the standing elbow); upper cabinets 450 to 600 mm above the worktop.
- **Sofa seat** 400 to 450 mm; **mattress top** 450 to 550 mm; **coffee table** about 400 mm.
- **Clearances:** at least 900 mm circulation; 750 to 900 mm dining pull-out (1100 mm to walk behind a diner); 380 to 460 mm sofa to coffee table; 600 mm minimum beside a bed; about 600 mm per place setting; at least 1065 mm kitchen work aisle; work-triangle legs 1.2 to 2.7 m (total 4.0 to 7.9 m).
- **Reach zones:** primary reach 750 to 1500 mm above finished floor; eye and art line 1500 to 1600 mm; overhead storage at or below 2100 mm.
- **Daylight:** window area at least one eighth of the floor area; pendants 750 mm above the tabletop.
